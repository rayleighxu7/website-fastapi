Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 2026

What is collected

This website collects a small amount of first-party analytics to understand traffic patterns and site usage.

  • Page visit events
  • Button click events for contact and project interactions
  • Technical metadata such as event path, timestamp, and click target label
  • Raw IP address stored temporarily for abuse prevention and analytics quality checks
  • A salted, one-way hashed visitor identifier derived from request IP and user-agent

What is not collected

  • No advertising trackers
  • No sale of personal information
  • No use of analytics data for ad targeting or profiling

Why this data is used

  • Understand which sections and project/contact actions are useful
  • Detect bot/noise traffic patterns
  • Improve site content and user experience

Retention

Raw IP addresses are automatically removed after 30 days. A hashed version of your IP, along with your click history, is retained indefinitely to recognize returning visitors over time.

Sharing

This data is not sold. It is stored in the site owner's analytics records and processed only by infrastructure providers needed to run this website (for example, hosting and database services).

Your options

Email rayleighxu7@live.com to request access or deletion. For visits older than 30 days, include the IP address used at the time so a hash comparison can be attempted against stored hashed records.
